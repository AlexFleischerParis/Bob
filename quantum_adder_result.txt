Quantum Adder Using CNOT and Toffoli Gates
======================================================================

This quantum adder uses:
- CNOT (CX) gates for XOR operations (computing sum bits)
- Toffoli (CCX) gates for AND operations (computing carry bits)
- Implements a ripple-carry adder algorithm

It can add two numbers between 0 and 7.
